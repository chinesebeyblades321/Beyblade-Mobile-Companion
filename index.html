Tap to play — rematchHow it works

Compare shows what really happened in S2, plus the predictor’s win % — that % is a chance, not a result. A “rematch” can’t change any real record, so this runs a whole simulated series for the pair picked above: every match is played round by round to first-to-5, finishes are drawn from each bey’s real finish mix (Spin 1 pt · KO 2 · Burst 3 · Extreme 5, ties 1 pt each), and round wins are tuned so the match odds land on that win % — so a KO-heavy bey needs fewer rounds to bank 5. Then you get the full breakdown. Every run is labelled Simulated and nothing here touches the real standings.
